\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

% VALUE: what the evaluation establishes, then the order: OPCORD shortens prefill and decode on five LLMs against the existing program on its own best shipped split; its choice of order and split stays near the best measured option; the gain follows the condition's shape; the event model, legality, and the census close the section
% DECISION: no question list at the head of the evaluation; subsections have noun titles and each opens with its finding.
% COMMENT: every \E value comes from the amendment-1 batch, the decode development batch, the synthetic sweep, the legality control, the FastFlowLM replay, and finally the final sweep; prose stays as written while numbers move (STYLE.md, Results vary between runs).
In this section, we show that OPCORD shortens prefill and decode on five LLMs against the existing program on the shipped split that minimizes its TTFT, that its choice of order and split stays close to the measured minimum, and that its gains follow the shape that the condition of Section~\ref{sec:condition} predicts.
After the setup, we report end-to-end latency, the quality of the choices, the shape of the gain, the sources of the gains, the event model's accuracy, legality, the census of the stacks we read, and the overheads.

\subsection{Shape of the Gain}
\label{subsec:shape-eval}

% VALUE: at one split, the reorder's gain follows the shape the condition predicts on every LLM: a tie at one chunk, a loss at two, a gain from four chunks that rises to about twice at 8-16K and falls at 32K; with many chunks an estimate from the two groups' busy times and the hidden host time tracks the measured gain, while at few chunks only the event model prices the per-command costs
At one split, the reorder's gain follows the shape that Section~\ref{subsec:exposure} predicts on every LLM.
It ties at one chunk, loses $0.945$--$0.972\times$ at two, gains from four chunks, and rises to about $2\times$ at 8--16K tokens before it falls at 32K.
At prompts of 1{,}024 to 4{,}096 tokens, the estimate from the two groups' busy times and the host time the overlap hides lies within 5\% of the measured gain on 23 of 24 configurations.
It overstates the gain at few chunks, where the per-command costs and the pipeline's fill and drain outweigh the overlap, and at long prompts where the groups balance, since two groups that overlap inside one command still fall short of hiding the shorter one entirely, which only the event model's composition of each command captures.

\subsection{Sources of the Gains}
\label{subsec:ablation}

% VALUE: at OPCORD's choices, nearly all of the prefill gain is the composer's reorder across column groups: the stream pass changes nothing at four columns and finds no move at one, and at the default two-column split it contributes a within-operator share that grows with the prompt; packing alone contributes nothing; decode's gain needs the whole cycle renamed
At the splits OPCORD chooses, nearly all of the prefill gain comes from the composer's reorder across column groups.
The stream pass changes nothing on the four-column split, whose attention stream already issues each column's work before it waits, and finds no move on the one-column split.
On the default two-column split, the stream pass alone gains \E{1.21--1.60}$\times$ at 16K tokens over the existing program, and the composer adds the reorder across groups on top.
None of the gain comes from packing alone (Section~\ref{subsec:semantics}).
% SOURCE: at the picks the stream arm measures 1.000 at S4 and the pass makes no move at S1 (amd-npu s4-cells, s1-cells NOTES; vault decisions log, issue check); stream-pass-only at S2 1.21-1.60x at 16K on three LLMs, a same-split figure, pre-final (development grids).

% VALUE: decode's gain needs the whole cycle renamed: keys and values alone gain nothing while the score buffer closes the cycle
In decode, renaming the keys and values alone, as a production mapping of another LLM does by hand~\citep{du2026gemma3}, is worth 0.3\%, because the shared score buffer still closes the loop's critical cycle, as the condition of Section~\ref{subsec:removal} requires.
% SOURCE: keys and values renamed with the existing score buffer kept: 1.002x (16,384) and 1.003x (32,512), tokens and logits bit-identical (vault lab-decode-long-context (28); amd-npu d8f0bd4c, score 5e1017a5); all three renamed (the loop pass): 1.269x / 1.391x (lab-decode-long-context (22)).

\subsection{Legality}
\label{subsec:legality-eval}

% VALUE: the checkers refuse every injected illegal rewrite; a rewrite refused on a single data hazard, forced onto the device, corrupts output while its accepted twin stays bit-identical, so a refusal marks a real hazard rather than caution; the two controls of the condition, run through the generic composer, measure about one, as the condition predicts
The checkers refuse every illegal rewrite we inject, and a refusal marks a real hazard rather than caution.

\subsection{Census of the Stacks}
\label{subsec:census}

% VALUE: the method reads exactly what each stack dispatches: streams of three toolchains re-emit byte for byte, nearly every wait is attributed to a mechanism, and every loop of the MLIR-AIR designs is judged
Because the method starts from artifacts a stack has already emitted, it must read them exactly, and it does so on all three stacks.
Re-emitting 625 streams byte for byte shows that the analysis starts from exactly what each stack dispatches, namely 375 streams built by MLIR-AIR and 218 recorded from FastFlowLM, both through the lifter of Section~\ref{subsec:closed}, and 32 built by IRON through IRON's own translator.
Over 792 streams, the analysis attributes 73{,}994 of 74{,}010 waits to one of its ordering mechanisms, and at loop granularity it judges all 3{,}719 loops of the MLIR-AIR prefill and decode designs.
% SOURCE: round trips (vault lab-cosched-passes (3), (9), (14)); attribution aie-cosched results/2026-10-23-s35-data (registered c57b708, result ef6733a); loops 3dbc1ee (MLIR-AIR prefill 3,488 / 3,488, the nine fused decodes 231 / 231).

% VALUE: across the census, the condition holds where gains are measured and explains where they are small: FastFlowLM's flash prefill exposes most of its time in multi-operator commands with nothing inside them to overlap, its waits between commands are mostly true, and the only rewrite its bound leaves worth measuring is merging commands at the shortest prompts, which measures \E; its decode bounds at about one; IRON's designs price at one
Across the census, the exposure and the bound of Section~\ref{subsec:exposure} explain where the gains are small.
On FastFlowLM's flash prefill, 82--87\% of the time lies in multi-operator commands whose false orders bound at $1.000\times$, and the false orders between its commands bound below $1.05\times$ on measured terms on every scored request.
The one rewrite its bound leaves worth measuring merges two of its commands at prompts of up to 128 tokens to save their dispatch, and replayed on the device it saves \E{X}\%.
Its decode bounds at $1.000$--$1.009\times$, and every one of IRON's 35 streams prices at $1.000\times$, since its streams issue their transfers ahead of their waits and each design runs one operator per configuration.

\subsection{Overheads}
\label{subsec:overheads}

% VALUE: OPCORD's costs are paid once per prompt length, LLM, and split, outside the measured call except a small binding step every TTFT includes; probing is once per LLM and split; the tie-break measurement is once per prompt length
OPCORD's costs are paid once per prompt length, LLM, and split, and later prompts of that length reuse its work.
Composing a length's commands takes \E{56--368}\,ms and planning it \E{0.06--1.1}\,s, and settling near-ties by measurement takes \E{X}\,s per length, about 19 minutes over the whole sweep.
Every TTFT includes the 7--50\,ms that binding attention's positions takes inside the call, and probing each operator alone costs 9--39\,s once per LLM and split.
% SOURCE: composition host-only (amd-npu 82c5b5b0, compose-gate-2026-10-22): S2 1B 56-148 ms, Qwen3-4B 143-368 ms; planning 63-206 ms (1B) and 0.3-1.1 s (Qwen3-4B); bind step 7-50 ms; probes 9-39 s per model and split (vault lab-split-sweep (29)-(30)); measure-when-close 1-275 s per shape, 1,144 s over the grid (npu-gap tmp/amend1/close-cost.txt).
